ABOUT THIS ARCHIVE

A history worth
understanding.

LOST WORLD is a selected, expandable encyclopedia of extinct animals. It was created to make the evidence behind their lives and disappearances easier to explore.

Browse the encyclopedia →
01

Explore the record

Browse animal profiles, habitats, fossils, historical observations, and the places where evidence was found.

02

Read with care

Fossil dates, body sizes, and extinction causes can be estimates. Hypotheses and unknown details are identified instead of guessed.

03

Keep perspective

Extinct species have no living individuals. Endangered animals still do, and conservation can prevent further losses.

A useful distinction

Genetic research is not a resurrection.

Studying ancient DNA or editing a living relative does not mean an extinct species has been fully restored. Protecting living species remains essential.
